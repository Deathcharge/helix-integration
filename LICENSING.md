## Why MPL-2.0

MPL-2.0 is a file-level copyleft license suited to a reusable safety library:

- Anyone may use the package, including in commercial or proprietary products.
- Source files containing Samsarix Integration Guard code, and modifications to
  those files, remain available under MPL-2.0 when distributed.
- A larger application may use different terms for its separate files.
- License, copyright, patent, warranty, and liability notices in covered source
  code cannot be stripped.
- Contributors provide the copyright and patent grants stated in the license.

MPL-2.0 does not require user-interface attribution and does not reserve a
commercial-use monopoly. The separate [NOTICE](NOTICE) file makes ownership and
contact information easy to preserve, while [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) keeps
brand rights separate from the source-code license.

## Scope and historical revisions

The root license applies to the current source tree, including the unsupported
historical snapshot under `legacy/`, unless a file explicitly states different
terms. Package builds contain only the supported `samsarix_guard` code.

Changing the license for the current tree does not revoke permissions already
granted for copies of historical revisions under the license distributed with
those revisions. The current `0.3.0` release uses MPL-2.0.

## Contributions

Unless a contribution explicitly says otherwise before acceptance, it is submitted
under MPL-2.0, the same license as the project. Samsarix LLC does not require a
copyright assignment or contributor license agreement at this stage. That keeps
contribution terms simple, but it also means Samsarix LLC cannot unilaterally
relicense third-party contributions later.

If future dual licensing becomes a business requirement, obtain legal advice and
adopt an appropriate contributor agreement before accepting contributions that
